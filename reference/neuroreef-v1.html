Reading the reef

Every shape is one animal, pointing the way it is heading. Each has its own small neural network, copied with random mutations whenever it breeds. Nothing is scripted: hunters, herds and boom-and-bust cycles come out of the brains that survive.

Who is who

    Signals on screen

    • White flash a newborn, small at first and growing.
    • Grey and dim starving. It is close to dying.
    • Red-orange pop a kill: a hunter just caught something.
    • Green sparkle a birth.
    • Green ground living plants. Orange smudges are carrion that Gleaners eat and that rots back into soil.

    The food web

    Drifters and Browsers graze the plants. Stalkers hunt Drifters and Gleaners. Leviathans hunt Browsers and Stalkers. Gleaners eat the carrion everyone leaves behind. Watch the history lines in the species list: when hunters boom, their prey crash, then the hunters starve and the prey return.
